Never painted weights? Tap Start and I'll find the torn spots for you — no experience needed.
Getting your paint job into the game:
  1. Tap Export GLB — it downloads with your new weights baked in.
  2. Send the file to the pipeline (attach it in chat, or push it to the repo).
  3. The pipeline runs tools/rig-repair/probe_shoulders.py — renders your weights at 15/30/45/60/90° to prove zero webbing.
  4. If clean, it lands as the new model via PR. If not, you get the probe frames back showing where it still tears.
Tip: paint small, then drag the Angle slider — that's the whole loop. Fix what tears.
